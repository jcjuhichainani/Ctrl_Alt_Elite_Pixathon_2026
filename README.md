## 🎮 Gameplay & Verification Workflow

The verification process consists of two sequential biological resonance tests:

### 🚀 Intro Screen
- Displays the ambient dark cybernetic UI (`CIVITAS // CORE-OS 2076`).
- Team attribution for **Ctrl Alt Elite**.
- Action Button: **`PROVE YOUR SOUL`**.

---

### 🎨 Stage 1: Biometric Freehand Tracing
1. **Target Geometry:** A random geometric bound (*Circle*, *Ellipse*, *Oval*, or *Squircle*) is rendered dynamically at **60% to 70%** of the screen scale.
2. **Visible Boundaries:** The boundary guide remains subtly visible on screen for tactile reference.
3. **30-Second Timer:** Users have a strict 30-second window to complete their trace.
4. **Humanized Trajectory Analysis:**
   - Evaluates micro-jitter, velocity variance, tangential curvature, angular consistency, and end-point closure.
   - Ignores rigid pixel perfection—bot-like linear perfection is flagged as synthetic, while natural human micro-deviations are validated.

---

### 🎯 Stage 2: Dynamic Arc Containment
1. **Integrated Perimeter Target:** The active shape from Stage 1 carries over into Stage 2. A randomized red target arc sector (the "catch zone") is integrated directly into the shape's perimeter boundary.
2. **Center-Bouncing Kinetic Ball:**
   - A high-speed ball bounces continuously in a fixed, randomized directional vector between the shape's outer perimeter wall and its exact center point.
   - Perpetual energy conservation ensures crisp, uninterrupted trajectory bounces.
3. **Rotation Mechanics:**
   - The user rotates the outer shape structure (via Drag/Mouse, Touch, or Left/Right Keyboard Arrow keys).
   - The objective is to position the red arc zone directly beneath the point where the ball contacts the outer boundary.
4. **Success Condition:** Catch **5 consecutive perimeter bounces** inside the red arc sector to complete the verification.

---

### 🏆 Completion Screen
Upon successfully completing both stages, the system confirms organic biological sovereignty with the statement:
> **"YOU ARE SOULFUL"**

Available actions on completion:
- **EXPORT VERIFICATION:** Copies a simulated cryptographic quantum proof hash to the clipboard.
- **RE-TEST:** Restarts the verification sequence with a fresh randomized configuration.

## 🛠️ Controls & Inputs

- **Mouse / Touch:**
  - *Stage 1:* Drag to trace along the active shape boundary.
  - *Stage 2:* Drag around the canvas center to rotate the target container.
- **Keyboard (Stage 2):**
  - **Left Arrow (`←`) / `A`:** Rotate container counter-clockwise.
  - **Right Arrow (`→`) / `D`:** Rotate container clockwise.
